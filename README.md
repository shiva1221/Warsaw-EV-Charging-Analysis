Warsaw EV Charging Network Analysis
An end-to-end data analytics project exploring EV charging infrastructure across Warsaw, Poland, built on SQL and Power BI.

Project Overview
Warsaw is at an interesting inflection point for electric vehicle adoption, and this project was built to understand what the city's charging infrastructure actually looks like beneath the surface. The analysis covers how stations are distributed across districts, where demand is outpacing supply, how customers behave, and where the real revenue opportunities lie.
The work brings together SQL analysis across 14 business queries, interactive Power BI dashboards, and a complete business presentation with strategic recommendations.

Major Business Insights
Five districts have no charging infrastructure at all, despite meaningful projected EV growth in those areas. Ursynów and Wesoła together account for roughly 36% of total revenue. High-income districts are consistently under-served relative to demand, while mid-range districts are delivering the most stable returns. At a city level, Warsaw is facing a significant and growing infrastructure deficit.

Strategic Recommendations

Prioritize charging expansion in the five districts with zero current coverage
Accelerate DC Fast Charger deployment across the network
Introduce dynamic pricing during identified peak hours
Build loyalty programs targeting high-frequency users
Redirect capital from over-served districts toward higher-opportunity zones
